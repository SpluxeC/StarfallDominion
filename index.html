How to Play

1. Select Vega and colonize it. 2. Build mines, reactors, farms, and shipyards. Strategic Extractors harvest titanium, crystals, or gas depending on the world. 3. Resources and science accumulate automatically as time passes; use Pause or Speed to test. 4. Research technology to strengthen your empire. 5. Build fleets and send them along hyperlanes. 6. Capture rival systems to win. Negotiate a ceasefire to buy time. Saves are stored in this browser; export them for backup.
